FAQ

Key questions

Anything else? Ask us on a free consultation.

Why isn’t the price listed?+

Pricing is per verified meeting and depends on buyer access complexity: a Fortune 500 C-level takes more touchpoints than a mid-market director.

How do you protect our brand reputation?+

Every message is signed off before launch and sent from isolated domains, positioned as a peer-to-peer conversation, not a generic pitch.

What if a prospect doesn’t show up?+

No-shows and unqualified meetings are flagged, replaced and removed from the billing tally.

How soon will the first meetings appear?+

Usually between week four and week six after kick-off, depending on your market and buyer seniority.

Who handles replies and books meetings?+

A dedicated SDR replies, follows up and checks each prospect against the agreed criteria before booking.

What do you need from us to start?+

Your offer, the customers you want to reach and a few successful projects. Strategy, setup and booking are on us.

How are you different from other agencies?+

No retainers or vague activity reports — you pay only for qualified pipeline, with full visibility from Day 1.

How do you generate leads?+

Multi-channel outreach: email for reach, calls where they open doors, LinkedIn for trust — updated month to month.
